Delete Your Future Jobs Pro AI Account

We're sorry to see you go. Follow the instructions below to permanently delete your account and associated data.

📧 Request Account Deletion

Send an email from the address linked to your account to:

support@futurejobsproai.com

with the subject line: "Delete My Account"

In the email, please include your full name and the email address you used to register. No additional verification is required – we will reply to confirm the deletion.

🗑️ What Data Is Deleted

  • Your name and email address
  • All projects, tasks, schedules, and time entries
  • Job site photos and voice notes
  • GPS location history and clock‑in/out records
  • Chat messages and support tickets
  • Subscription and payment information (except anonymised records required by law)

⏱️ Retention Period

After we receive your request, we will delete your data within 30 days. You will receive a confirmation email once the deletion is complete.

🔒 What Data Is Kept (for legal compliance)

Anonymised logs for security and fraud prevention may be retained for up to 90 days. These cannot be used to identify you personally.

⚠️ Important
Account deletion is permanent. You will lose access to all your data, and your company may lose records associated with your account. If you are part of a team, please notify your manager before requesting deletion.

❓ Alternative: Delete Some Data Without Deleting Your Account

If you only want to remove certain data (e.g., photos or voice notes), you can do so directly in the app:

  • Photos – Open a project album, tap any photo, and use the trash icon.
  • Voice notes – Delete them from the voice note history screen.
  • Time entries – Managers can edit or delete incorrect entries.
